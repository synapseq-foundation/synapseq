2. **Separate commercial license** — for those who wish to incorporate SynapSeq

   commercial terms.

## How to obtain the commercial license

Contact [contact@synapseq.org](mailto:contact@synapseq.org). The typical process is:

1. Initial contact describing the intended use (closed product, distribution,
   volume).
2. Evaluation of the scope and a commercial proposal (price, scope, payment
   model).
3. Written acceptance (signed email or electronic contract).
4. Formal grant through a signed Commercial License agreement by both parties.
5. Contract registration and payment.

## Third-party contributions

As the copyright holder of the code, the author may grant commercial licenses
over the whole project. If there are third-party contributions in the
repository, a **CLA (Contributor License Agreement)** or copyright assignment
will be required before licensing the project commercially in its entirety.

By contributing to SynapSeq, you agree that your contribution may be licensed
both under the GPL v3 and under a separate commercial license granted by the
copyright holder. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and
[docs/CLA.md](CLA.md) for details.

## What to avoid

- Distributing closed derivatives without a valid commercial license (copyright
  infringement).
- Accepting external contributions without a CLA if there is a future plan for
  dual licensing.
- Promising commercial licenses informally by email alone, without written
  documentation.

Third-party dependencies in `vendor/` keep their own licenses and are not
covered by the SynapSeq commercial license. See
[docs/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md).
